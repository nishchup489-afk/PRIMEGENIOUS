Explanation

Pure theory

Prime numbers → only divisible by 1 and the number itself. Like: 2, 3, 5, 19, 73 ...

Factors → small numbers you multiply to get the big number.

12 = 1 × 12

12 = 1 × 2 × 6

12 = 1 × 2 × 2 × 3

So, prime factors → factors that are prime.

In case of 12 = 1 × 2 × 6, these are not prime factors because 6 is divisible, so we need to divide it further by a smaller prime like 2.

12 = 1 × 2 × 2 × 3

Now those are the prime factors.

Note

• 1 is not a prime number

• Smallest prime number is 2, so we start by counting from 2

• Even numbers are never prime numbers (except 2 itself)

If c = a × b

then √c ≥ a  and/or  √c ≥ b

That means once you pass the square root limit, you do not need to keep checking bigger factors.

How to get prime factors in math

Suppose the number is n = 60.

Step 1

Keep dividing by the smallest prime 2 until no more divisible. If divisible, 2 is in the list of prime factors.

602 = 30   →   [ 2 ]

302 = 15   →   [ 2, 2 ]

152 = ❌

Step 2

Move to the next smaller prime number 3 and keep dividing until you can.

153 = 5   →   [ 2, 2, 3 ]

Step 3

Check the square root limit of your number (optional in small cases or in math).

√60 = 7.7459 ≈ 7

So we only have to check factors up to 7.

Step 4

Keep dividing until no more divisible by any valid number.

Next prime: 5

55 = 1

Now 1 is no more divisible, so prime factors are [ 2, 2, 3, 5 ].

Case 2: 84

First smallest prime: 2

√84 = 9.17 ≈ 9

842 = 42   →   [ 2 ]

422 = 21   →   [ 2, 2 ]

212 = ❌

213 = 7   →   [ 2, 2, 3 ]

73 = ❌

75 = ❌

77 = 1   →   [ 2, 2, 3, 7 ]

We get 1, so no more divisible. PF = [ 2, 2, 3, 7 ]

In code

Remember: we exclude unnecessary cases to make the logic cleaner and faster.

To exclude

  1. 1. Take input and create a number list
  2. 2. Exclude all numbers divisible by 2 and append 2
  3. 3. Exclude all numbers divisible by 3 and append 3 (optional but good)
  4. 4. Start iteration from next prime i = 5
  5. 5. Exclude all numbers greater than square root of input
  6. 6. Exclude all iterations which are not divisible by input
  7. 7. Exclude even numbers using i += 2
  8. 8. Finally if the number itself remains, append it
